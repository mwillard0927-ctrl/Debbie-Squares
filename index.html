How to use

Debbie's Squares

The main screen

  • Each square is one week of the season, showing your 3 teams for that week.
  • The current week (or, between weeks, the next week coming up) is highlighted in yellow.
  • Final under a score means the game is over.
  • When a game is on right now, that week gets a red border and a flashing LIVE tag, and the game clock (like "2:00 - 2nd") shows in red under the score.
  • Scores with a ~ (like ~21) are projected points from the betting lines for games not played yet.
  • Total adds up your teams' points so far. If some games haven't been played yet, it also shows "+ ~N still to play". Projected is the expected total before any games are played.
  • BYE means that team doesn't play that week.

See a week up close

  • Tap any week to open it full screen.
  • You'll see each game: who your team played, both logos and records, the score, and whether it's final, live, or when it starts.
  • Upcoming games show How to watch, based on your 📺 My TV settings: the first line is how you can watch it; anything in orange is a channel you may not have, with 2 ways to watch.
  • Tap anywhere outside the box to close it.

Top of the screen

  • The black bar shows your next game: the matchup, how long until kickoff, and the channel. When a game is on, it shows the live score instead.
  • Season total adds up all your points so far (for the Grand Prize), plus the projected points still to come. Tap it to see a chart of every week.

📺 My TV (how to watch)

  • Tap 📺 My TV (under the buttons) to pick the city you live in and check what you watch on: antenna, cable, live TV streaming, Prime Video, Netflix, Peacock, ESPN, Paramount+, NFL+, Sunday Ticket.
  • The app then shows each game's local channel, whether it's free with an antenna, and whether you can watch it with what you have.
  • Sunday 1 PM and 4 PM CBS/FOX games are regional: they show in some cities and not others. Games with your city's home team are always shown locally.
  • No cable or streaming? A basic TV antenna (about $25) gets CBS, FOX, NBC and ABC free.
  • Debbie's Squares is already set for Detroit with an antenna, Netflix and Prime. New squares ask for this right after you save them.

Game alerts

  • The first time you open the app, it asks "Turn on game alerts?" Tap Yes, then Allow when your phone asks.
  • Missed it or tapped Not now? Tap 🔔 Game alerts under the buttons any time, then Allow. Tap it again to turn alerts off.
  • You'll get a buzz when your team kicks off, scores, and when the game is final, plus a weekly heads-up listing your games, times and channels.
  • Alerts come even when the app is closed.
  • Android: works right from Chrome. Adding the app to your home screen (below) helps alerts arrive reliably.
  • iPhone: alerts only work from the Home Screen app (iOS 16.4 or newer): in Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen → Add, open Squares from the new icon, then turn alerts on there. The app shows you these steps the first time you open it in Safari.
  • Not getting alerts? Check that notifications are allowed for the app in your phone's Settings.

Enter the high & low winners

  • After a week is over, press and hold its square for about half a second.
  • Type the winning HIGH score and tap Save, then the winning LOW score and tap Save.
  • Tap Skip to leave one unchanged. Hold the square again any time to fix a number.
  • These are saved on this phone only.
  • Once entered, the square shows how close you came, like "🏆 Won the low!" or "8 from the low".
  • The season chart (tap Season total) shows every week's total next to the winning high and low.

Share the app

  • Tap the red Share button for a QR code someone can scan, a Copy button for the link, and a Share button to text it.

Scores & updates

  • Scores update by themselves every minute while the app is open.
  • The line under the grid shows when scores last updated. If it says Offline, you're seeing the last saved scores.
  • Works without internet. It just shows the last scores it saved.

Put it on your home screen

  • iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android (Chrome): tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen / Install app.
